Editorial and Advertising Policy

Last reviewed: July 18, 2026

Purpose

Aging Gracefully helps families discover home-modification contractors and senior-support services. Pages are written for people, not to manufacture search traffic. We do not promise rankings to providers or guarantee outcomes to visitors.

How listings are assembled

Basic listing details may come from publicly available business information and provider submissions. We aim to show useful contact and service information, but inclusion is not an endorsement. Providers may request a free basic listing or correction. We may decline irrelevant, misleading, duplicate, unsafe, or unverifiable submissions.

Verification and ratings

We do not call a provider “verified” unless we describe the specific check performed. Visitors should independently confirm licenses, insurance, permits, credentials, and references. We do not currently display third-party ratings or review counts because their source, retrieval date, required attribution, and refresh cadence have not been verified.

Editorial standards

  • Local pages should contain materially useful information relevant to that place—not just city-name substitutions.
  • Health, benefits, cost, and safety statements should be framed carefully and linked to authoritative sources when practical.
  • Material errors are corrected without requiring payment.
  • Provider descriptions should be factual and should not contain unsupported superlatives or guarantees.

Advertising Policy

Aging Gracefully is committed to transparency regarding its advertising practices. Our goal is to provide valuable information to users while maintaining a clear distinction between editorial content and paid placements.

Types of Advertising

  • Display Advertisements: These are clearly labeled graphical ads served by third-party platforms (e.g., Google AdSense). Their placement and content are managed by the advertising networks, and we do not endorse the advertised products or services.
  • Sponsored Placements: Providers may pay for enhanced visibility or featured listings within our directory. Such placements are always clearly identified with labels like "Sponsored" or "Featured" to ensure users understand their commercial nature. Payment for sponsored placement does not influence a provider's eligibility for a basic listing, nor does it affect our editorial standards for accuracy or our correction policy.
  • Affiliate Links: Some content may include affiliate links to products or services. If a user clicks on an affiliate link and makes a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to the user. These links are disclosed, and their inclusion does not compromise the impartiality of our editorial recommendations.

Advertising Principles

  • Clear Labeling: All forms of advertising and sponsored content are clearly and unambiguously labeled to distinguish them from independent editorial content.
  • Editorial Independence: Advertising revenue does not influence our editorial integrity, content creation, or the impartial application of our correction and removal policies.
  • Relevance: We strive to ensure that advertising content is relevant to our audience and the themes of aging-in-place and senior services.
  • No Endorsement: Unless explicitly stated, the presence of an advertisement or sponsored placement does not constitute an endorsement or recommendation by Aging Gracefully. Users should exercise their own judgment and conduct due diligence when engaging with advertisers.
