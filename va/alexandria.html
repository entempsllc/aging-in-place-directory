Navigating Old Town Sidewalks & Historic Permits

Modifying a home in a historic district like Old Town Alexandria often requires specific approval from the Board of Architectural Review (BAR). Ensure your contractor is familiar with local historic preservation guidelines before starting external work like ramps or stairlift rails visible from the street.

Five checks before hiring

  1. Confirm the company serves your address and has experience with the specific modification you need.
  2. Verify licenses with the Virginia Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation (DPOR).
  3. Request a written, itemized scope covering labor, materials, permits, warranty terms, payment schedule, and change orders.
  4. Check recent references for comparable accessibility or aging-in-place work. A high online rating alone is not proof of relevant experience.
  5. For larger accessibility projects, ask who will assess fit and function and whether a CAPS professional, occupational therapist, or other qualified specialist should be involved.

Official starting points: the FTC home-improvement scam guide, the federal Eldercare Locator, and the NAHB explanation of the CAPS credential.

Local help and funding in Alexandria

The Virginia Department for Aging and Rehabilitative Services (800-552-3404) serves Virginia residents. Visit https://vda.virginia.gov/ or call to ask about home modification programs, minor repair services, or grant-funded safety modifications for eligible seniors.

Other potential funding sources include the VA HISA grant for eligible veterans, Medicaid HCBS waivers for eligible beneficiaries, and the USDA Section 504 program for very-low-income rural homeowners. Call 211 for local social services and home repair programs.

Aging in place in Alexandria's historic homes

Alexandria's historic housing stock in Old Town and Del Ray often requires careful aging-in-place modifications that preserve character while adding accessibility. Bathroom grab bars ($200 to $350 per bar) are the most common request, followed by tub-to-shower conversions ($1,500 to $15,000) and comfort-height toilets ($350 to $900). Many Alexandria homes have step-up entries that benefit from threshold ramps or modular aluminum ramps ($1,100 to $5,500). The Virginia Department for Aging and Rehabilitative Services (800-552-3404) can help identify local programs.

How to choose a contractor in Alexandria

  1. Look for a CAPS-certified contractor. Search the NAHB CAPS directory for a Certified Aging-in-Place Specialist in Virginia.
  2. Verify licensing and insurance. Check your state's contractor license lookup and confirm liability insurance and workers' comp coverage.
  3. Get three written, itemized quotes. Compare scope, not just total.
  4. Ask about references from senior clients. Two or three past clients with similar modifications.
  5. Consider an OT assessment. See our CAPS specialist guide for more.

Frequently asked questions — Alexandria

What does it cost to install grab bars in Alexandria?

Professional grab bar installation costs $200 to $350 per bar, with labor at $50 to $125 per hour and a two-hour minimum for most jobs. Installing several bars in one visit lowers the per-bar cost.

Does Virginia offer financial help for home modifications for seniors?

The Virginia Department for Aging and Rehabilitative Services (800-552-3404) may offer home modification or minor repair programs. Contact them to ask about eligibility and available services in your area.

How do I find a CAPS-certified contractor in Alexandria?

Search the NAHB directory at nahb.org/education/find-an-education-graduate. Select Virginia and the CAPS designation. Verify any contractor through your state's licensing board.

Do I need a permit for a bathroom remodel in Alexandria?

A tub-to-shower conversion that alters plumbing typically requires a plumbing permit. Permit fees generally run $250 to $500. Your contractor should pull the permit and arrange inspections.

What should I consider for aging in place in an Alexandria historic home?

Alexandria's historic homes in Old Town and Del Ray may have narrow doorways and small bathrooms. Common modifications include grab bars ($200 to $350 each), swing-clear hinges for doorways ($200 per door), and compact walk-in showers. Work with a contractor experienced in historic homes to preserve character while adding accessibility.

What is the most important aging-in-place modification for an Alexandria home?

Install grab bars in the bathroom ($200 to $350 each). Alexandria's historic homes often have small bathrooms where a grab bar at the toilet and shower entry makes the biggest difference. If the home has a step-up front entry, add a threshold ramp ($200 to $500). These two modifications together address the highest fall risks.
