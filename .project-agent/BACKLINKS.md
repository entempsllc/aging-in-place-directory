## Prohibited tactics

- Buying links or paying for undisclosed placement
- Automated comments, forum spam, or mass email
- Fake people, accounts, reviews, organizations, or testimonials
- Private blog networks or expired-domain schemes
- Forced or irrelevant reciprocal links
- Mass low-quality directory submissions
- Misleading widgets, badges, or embeds
- Keyword-stuffed anchors
- Copying the same pitch or asset across unrelated projects
- Cross-linking Paul's sites unless the link is editorially useful to the reader and contextually relevant
- Any action violating Google spam policies or a platform's terms
