## Free Senior-Resource Directories

Submit the site to any that accept free submissions:

- [ ] **Eldercare Locator** — https://eldercare.acl.gov/ (contact to be listed as a resource)
- [ ] **AARP Local Resources** — https://www.aarp.org/local-resources/ (submit as a local resource)
- [ ] **Benefits.gov** — https://www.benefits.gov/ (check if aging-in-place resources can be listed)
- [ ] **211.org** — https://www.211.org/ (contact local 211 to be added as a resource)
- [ ] **Area Agencies on Aging** — Contact each of the 10 priority city AAAs and ask to be listed on their resource pages (see targets.csv)
- [ ] **NAHB CAPS Directory** — Link from our CAPS guide to the NAHB directory; ask NAHB to link back
- [ ] **National Council on Aging** — https://www.ncoa.org/ (contact about being listed as a resource)
- [ ] **Care.com** — https://www.care.com/ (submit as a senior care resource)
- [ ] **Caring.com** — https://www.caring.com/ (local resources section)
- [ ] **SeniorLiving.org** — https://www.seniorliving.org/ (resource directory submission)
