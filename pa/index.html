State funding and resources for Pennsylvania

The Pennsylvania Department of Aging serves Pennsylvania seniors. Contact them at 717-783-1550 or visit https://www.aging.pa.gov/ to ask about home modification programs, minor repair services, or grant-funded safety modifications.

Other funding sources include the VA HISA grant for eligible veterans, Medicaid HCBS waivers for eligible beneficiaries, and the USDA Section 504 program. Call 211 for local social services.
