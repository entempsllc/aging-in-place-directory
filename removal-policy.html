Provider Removal Policy

Providers, authorized representatives, or individuals may request the removal of a listing or personal contact information from the Aging Gracefully directory.

Reasons for Removal

We evaluate removal requests based on several factors, including but not limited to:

  • Inaccuracy or outdated information that cannot be corrected.
  • Cessation of business operations.
  • Privacy concerns related to publicly available personal contact information, where applicable law or common practice supports removal.
  • Legal obligations requiring the suppression of information.

What to include in your request

  • The exact URL of the listing or page you wish to have removed.
  • The specific name of the provider or individual as it appears on our site.
  • A clear, concise reason for the removal request.
  • Any supporting documentation (e.g., business closure notice, court order), without including sensitive personal data unless explicitly required and anonymized.
  • Your relationship to the provider or individual.

Email a removal request

Our Process

Upon receiving a removal request, we will review the provided information and may attempt to verify the details using public sources. We reserve the right to decline requests that do not meet our criteria or are not supported by sufficient evidence. Our decisions are made impartially and are not influenced by any advertising agreements.

We will notify the requester of our decision and any actions taken. We aim to process all legitimate requests promptly.
