Aging-in-Place Services & Planning in Atlanta, GA

Navigating home modifications, home care, and senior resources across the multi-county Atlanta Metro.

11.2% of Atlanta residents are 65 or older, according to the U.S. Census Bureau QuickFacts. Common aging-in-place modifications in Atlanta include bathroom grab bars, walk-in shower conversions, and entry ramps for the city's many mid-century ranch homes.

Financial Assistance in Georgia

Georgia offers several programs for low-income seniors. The Empowerline system (from the Atlanta Regional Commission) is the primary gateway for aging services in the 10-county region. You may be eligible for the Community Care Services Program (CCSP), which can sometimes provide home-delivered services and certain modifications.

Five checks before hiring

  1. Confirm the company serves your specific county in the Atlanta metro area.
  2. Verify licenses with the Georgia State Board of Residential and General Contractors.
  3. Request a written, itemized scope covering labor, materials, permits, warranty terms, payment schedule, and change orders.
  4. Check recent references for comparable accessibility or aging-in-place work. A high online rating alone is not proof of relevant experience.
  5. For larger accessibility projects, ask who will assess fit and function and whether a CAPS professional, occupational therapist, or other qualified specialist should be involved.

Official starting points: the FTC home-improvement scam guide, the federal Eldercare Locator, and the NAHB explanation of the CAPS credential.

Frequently asked questions — Atlanta

What is Empowerline?

Empowerline is the official resource for the Atlanta Area Agency on Aging (AAA). It provides a central point of entry for information on aging and disability services in the 10-county Atlanta region.

Does Atlanta have specialized senior transportation?

Yes, many counties offer paratransit services. MARTA Mobility provides curb-to-curb transportation for people with disabilities, and many county senior centers offer localized transit options for residents.

What are the most common aging-in-place modifications for Atlanta homes?

The most common requests are bathroom grab bars ($200 to $350 per bar), tub-to-shower conversions ($1,500 to $15,000), and entry ramps ($1,100 to $5,500). Many Atlanta homes have step-up front entries that benefit from threshold or modular ramps.

Local help and funding in Atlanta

The Georgia Division of Aging Services (404-657-5310) serves Georgia residents. Visit https://aging.dhs.georgia.gov/ or call to ask about home modification programs, minor repair services, or grant-funded safety modifications for eligible seniors.

Other potential funding sources include the VA HISA grant for eligible veterans, Medicaid HCBS waivers for eligible beneficiaries, and the USDA Section 504 program for very-low-income rural homeowners. Call 211 for local social services and home repair programs.

Common aging-in-place modifications in Atlanta

Atlanta's housing stock ranges from historic Craftsman bungalows in neighborhoods like Inman Park and Grant Park to newer construction in the northern suburbs. The most common aging-in-place requests are bathroom grab bars ($200 to $350 per bar), tub-to-shower conversions ($1,500 to $15,000), and entry ramps for homes with step-up front entries. Atlanta's humidity makes non-slip flooring especially important in bathrooms and entryways. Many Atlanta-area contractors serve the broader metro including Sandy Springs, Marietta, and Decatur.

How to choose a contractor in Atlanta

  1. Look for a CAPS-certified contractor. Search the NAHB CAPS directory for a Certified Aging-in-Place Specialist in Georgia.
  2. Verify licensing and insurance. Check your state's contractor license lookup and confirm liability insurance and workers' comp coverage.
  3. Get three written, itemized quotes. Compare scope, not just total.
  4. Ask about references from senior clients. Two or three past clients with similar modifications.
  5. Consider an OT assessment. See our CAPS specialist guide for more.

Frequently asked questions — Atlanta

What does it cost to install grab bars in Atlanta?

Professional grab bar installation costs $200 to $350 per bar, with labor at $50 to $125 per hour and a two-hour minimum for most jobs. Installing several bars in one visit lowers the per-bar cost.

Does Georgia offer financial help for home modifications for seniors?

The Georgia Division of Aging Services (404-657-5310) may offer home modification or minor repair programs. Contact them to ask about eligibility and available services in your area.

How do I find a CAPS-certified contractor in Atlanta?

Search the NAHB directory at nahb.org/education/find-an-education-graduate. Select Georgia and the CAPS designation. Verify any contractor through your state's licensing board.

Do I need a permit for a bathroom remodel in Atlanta?

A tub-to-shower conversion that alters plumbing typically requires a plumbing permit. Permit fees generally run $250 to $500. Your contractor should pull the permit and arrange inspections.
