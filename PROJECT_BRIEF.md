## Purpose and audience

Aging Gracefully helps older adults and family caregivers organize practical aging-in-place decisions, find public planning resources, and research local providers. It covers home modifications, accessibility equipment, home care and daily living, transportation, social connection/day programs, and elder-law/financial planning. Content is organizational and educational, not medical, legal, or financial advice.

Success means visitors reach useful source-backed guidance, can compare relevant local research leads, and can submit attributable requests for appropriate local providers without unsupported endorsement or response promises.

## Offering and content pillars

1. **Local provider discovery:** curated city directories, categories, public contact details, and verification/hiring checklists.
2. **Aging-in-place planning:** home safety and accessibility decision support.
3. **Daily living and care coordination:** organizational guides for home care, meals, medication routines, and choosing help; no diagnosis.
4. **Transportation and social connection:** practical planning resources and official program links.
5. **Financial and legal organization:** questions, documents, and authoritative referral sources; no individualized advice.

## Trust and brand

The brand is calm, practical, accessible, and explicit about uncertainty. Listings are research leads, not endorsements. Do not claim providers are trusted, verified, licensed, insured, certified, or available without recorded evidence. Keep health and elder-care material source-backed and organizational. Preserve corrections, editorial/advertising, privacy, terms, publisher identity, and accessible presentation.
