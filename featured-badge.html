Featured on AgingGracefully.care — Badge

Verified aging-in-place contractors listed on our directory can display this badge on their website. It signals independent listing and links back to your city page.

How it works

If your company is listed on AgingGracefully.care, you can display the "Featured on AgingGracefully.care" badge on your website. The badge is a simple SVG image with a link back to your city page on our directory. There is no cost and no ongoing obligation.

Copy this code

Replace [YOUR-CITY-URL] with your actual city page URL (e.g., https://www.agingracefully.care/oh/columbus.html):

To display the badge, your company must be actively listed on AgingGracefully.care with verified contact information. Contact us at entempsllc@gmail.com if you'd like to be listed or if you need help installing the badge.
