Provider advertising pilot

A clearly labeled enhanced profile for $75.

The offer covers all 35 supported locations and is open to qualified aging-in-place and accessibility providers that genuinely serve one or more of them. We are accepting the first three qualified providers across the directory.

See exactly what is included

Pilot terms

  • $75 one-time pilot price for one Sponsored Enhanced Profile.
  • 30 consecutive days, beginning only after the profile is published and checked on the live site.
  • Available to the first three qualified providers across all supported markets.
  • No automatic renewal. Any renewal requires a new written agreement.
  • No payment is collected on this page. We first confirm eligibility, profile content, rights to supplied media, dates, and terms.

What is included

  • A visibly labeled Sponsored Enhanced Profile on one relevant city page and service category.
  • Factual business name, service area, contact details, service summary, website, and one provider-supplied logo or image when usage permission is confirmed.
  • One factual correction or copy revision during the 30-day placement.
  • A privacy-minimal attributable outbound link. Sponsored outbound links use nofollow sponsored attributes.
  • An end-of-term report containing only measurements actually collected, such as placement dates, availability checks, and attributable clicks or inquiries when available.

Required sponsorship disclosure

Sponsored Enhanced Profile: This provider paid for enhanced placement. Payment affects presentation and prominence, not ordinary listing eligibility, factual corrections, or our independent editorial standards. Sponsorship does not imply endorsement, verification, availability, or guaranteed results.

What the pilot does not promise

Aging Gracefully does not guarantee impressions, clicks, inquiries, leads, rankings, appointments, or sales. Payment cannot purchase a favorable review, credential claim, category exclusivity, suppression of accurate corrections, or control over ordinary listings.

Basic listings and factual corrections remain free. A provider does not have to purchase sponsorship to request an eligible basic listing or correct material information.

Who may qualify

The initial pilot is intended for providers that directly serve older adults, disabled residents, caregivers, or families with relevant home-accessibility or aging-in-place services. We check geographic fit, category relevance, a functioning public business presence, and whether profile claims can be supported. We may decline misleading, irrelevant, unsafe, duplicate, or unverifiable submissions.
